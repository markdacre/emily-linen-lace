How we work
— Three steps. No templates.

A plan that's unhurried,
and unmistakably yours.

01/03

We talk first.

A free twenty-minute call — in English or Portuguese. You tell me what's going on: the knee, the shoulder, the climb up the Boca do Inferno you used to do without thinking. I listen, ask a lot of questions, and tell you honestly whether I'm the right person to help.

02/03

I build your plan.

Written for your body, your history, and the thing you're trying to get back to — never a template. If a doctor or physio said just be careful or don't do that anymore, we start there together. Then we work, gently and stubbornly, back toward it.

03/03

We get to work.

In the studio, online, or some combination of both. I adjust the plan week by week based on what your body is telling us. My job is to make you more capable than you were — and, eventually, to get out of your way.

The Atlantic is right there.
Let's use it.

About Hi, I'm Emily.

I grew up in Northern California — the kind of childhood spent running on trails until the sun went down. I trained as a coach in Oakland, worked in clinics in Marin and the East Bay, and in 2022 I moved my practice to Cascais, where my husband's family is from and where the air smells like pine and salt at the same time.

I've spent over a decade coaching people of every age and ability — athletes, busy parents, folks rebuilding after surgery, and anyone who wants to get stronger without getting hurt. My training is precise, adaptable, and stubbornly patient. The goal is always the same: make you more capable in your own life.
